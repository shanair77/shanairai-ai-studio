**The idea of the episode:** a city where the wild comes right to the edge of town. Penguins on the beach, a whole floral kingdom on the hillsides, whales in the bay, and, out in that same bay, the island that held Nelson Mandela. It could also be **the end of the road**: the season has travelled from the top of Africa (Morocco) to nearly the bottom.

As with the last three episodes, **every fact below was checked before writing** (sources at the end).

## Beats

### 0 · Hook — CODE *(decision 1)*
- **Option A — Penguins on an African beach (recommended):** a pen draws a sunny beach with granite boulders; little penguins waddle in one by one; a scribbled "Africa?!" appears beside them.
  - **Narration:** "On this African beach, you share the sand with penguins."
  - **On screen:** "Boulders Beach · Cape Town" written in as the last penguin arrives.
  - Why: the most surprising image of the trip, and very shareable.
- **Option B — The tablecloth:** a pen draws flat-topped Table Mountain; a white cloud pours over the edge like a cloth over a table. "Some days, this mountain lays its own tablecloth."
- **Option C — A whole floral kingdom:** the world map shows six floral kingdoms; five are huge, and the sixth is a tiny patch at the tip of Africa. "This tiny corner of Africa is one of the world's six floral kingdoms."

### 1 · Title — CODE
- **Picture:** The Africa map draws in; the season route runs Morocco → … → Namibia → Cape Town; the stamp lands.
- **Narration:** "This is Postcards from Africa. Stop seven: Cape Town."
- **On screen:** Location card with **South Africa's flag**; stamp "STOP 07 · CAPE TOWN".

### 2 · Table Mountain — AI-K (hero)
- **Picture:** The traveler, seen from behind, stands at the edge of the flat summit looking down over the city and the bay; a white cloud spills over the cliff edge beside her; a cable car glides up below.
- **Narration:** "Table Mountain rises more than a thousand metres over the city, and a cable car takes you to its flat top in about five minutes."
- **Fact card:** "When cloud spills over its edge, Capetonians call it the 'tablecloth'."

### 3 · Fynbos — LAYER (push)
- **Picture:** A mountainside of fynbos in bloom: giant king proteas, pincushions and heather in pinks, oranges and greens; the sea far below.
- **Narration:** "The hills around the Cape are covered in fynbos, part of one of the world's six floral kingdoms, and the smallest of them all."
- **Fact card:** "About 9,000 plant species grow here, and roughly 70% grow nowhere else on Earth."

### 4 · Boulders Beach — AI-K (hero)
- **Picture:** The traveler, seen from behind, stands on a wooden boardwalk watching African penguins waddle between big granite boulders and splash into turquoise water.
- **Narration:** "At Boulders Beach, African penguins nest among the granite boulders, just outside town."
- **Fact card:** "In 2024 the African penguin was listed as Critically Endangered. Stay on the boardwalks and keep your distance."

### 5 · The Cape of Good Hope — LAYER
- **Picture:** Towering sea cliffs at Cape Point, an old lighthouse on the headland, waves crashing far below.
- **Narration:** "The Cape of Good Hope feels like the end of Africa. But the continent's true southern tip is Cape Agulhas, further east along the coast."
- **Fact card:** "Cape Agulhas lies about 55 kilometres further south. It's also where the warm Indian and cold Atlantic currents meet, more or less."

### 6 · Bo-Kaap — LAYER
- **Picture:** A steep cobbled street of brightly painted houses in turquoise, pink, yellow and lime, a mosque minaret, Signal Hill behind; the traveler small on the street.
- **Narration:** "In the Bo-Kaap, brightly painted houses line the cobbled streets of the city's historic Cape Malay quarter."
- **Fact card:** "The bright colours are a fairly recent tradition, linked to repainting homes before Eid."
- **Care notes:** this is a lived-in neighbourhood; the painting shows the street, not residents posed for tourists.

### 7 · History beat *(decision 2)*
- **Option A — Robben Island (recommended):** LAYER. Robben Island low on the horizon across the blue bay, a ferry crossing towards it, Table Mountain behind. "Out in the bay lies Robben Island, where Nelson Mandela spent eighteen of his twenty-seven years in prison." **Local tip:** "Tours are led by former political prisoners. Book well ahead."
- **Option B — District Six:** LAYER, with a **History card**. "In 1966, the apartheid government declared District Six a whites-only area, and over the next fifteen years more than 60,000 residents were forcibly removed." The District Six Museum keeps its story.
- **Option C — Both:** Robben Island as narration, District Six as a fact card on the Bo-Kaap beat.

### 8 · Whales — AI-M (atmosphere)
- **Picture:** A southern right whale breaching close to a rocky shore in a calm bay; a mother and calf surfacing nearby. No people.
- **Narration:** "From June to November, southern right whales come close to shore near Hermanus to have their calves."

### 9 · Sunset — AI-K (hero)
- **Picture:** The traveler, seen from behind, sits on a high rocky lookout at sunset; the city lights flicker on below, the Atlantic glows gold, Table Mountain darkens behind.
- **Narration:** "And at sunset, the whole city glows between the mountain and the sea."

### 10 · Traveler's notes — CODE
- **Notes:**
  - **Best time:** December–April for beaches and sun; June–November for whales, lower prices and fewer crowds.
  - **Money:** South African rand.
  - **Language:** 12 official languages; English is widely spoken.
  - **Book ahead:** Robben Island tours and the Table Mountain cable car (it closes in high wind).
  - **Respect:** stay on the penguin boardwalks, and ask before photographing people or homes.
- **Narration:** "Come in summer for beaches, in winter for whales, and book Robben Island well ahead."

### 11 · Next stop or season finale — CODE *(decision 3)*
- **Option A — Season finale (recommended):** the whole season route draws across the map from Morocco to Cape Town, every pin lights up, and the stamp reads "SEASON 1 · COMPLETE". "That's the end of Season 1. Thanks for travelling with us." The Season 1 video then ends on this page instead of the generic end card.
- **Option B — Keep going:** "Next stop: Madagascar." (or Mozambique, Mauritius…), and Season 1 continues.

**Narration:** about 240 words across 12 beats. **Estimated running time:** ~105–115 s.
