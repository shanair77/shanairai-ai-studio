## Beats

### 0 · Hook — CODE
- **Picture:** A trail of small footprints is pressed into the journal page one step at a time, as if into soft ash. A pen dates it: "~3.6 million years ago."
- **Narration:** "About three and a half million years ago, our early ancestors walked across this land, and their footprints are still here."
- **On screen:** "Laetoli, Tanzania" and the date, written in as the last footprint lands.

### 1 · Title — CODE
- **Picture:** The Africa map draws in; the season route runs Morocco → Egypt → Tanzania; the stamp lands.
- **Narration:** "This is Postcards from Africa. Stop three: Tanzania."
- **On screen:** Location card with Tanzania's flag; stamp "STOP 03 · TANZANIA".

### 2 · The Great Migration — AI-K (hero)
- **Picture:** Golden hour on the Serengeti. The traveler stands up through the open roof of a safari vehicle, seen from behind, watching an endless line of wildebeest and zebra stream across the plain.
- **Narration:** "In the Serengeti, around one and a half million wildebeest follow the rains in a giant loop that never ends."
- **Fact card:** "'Serengeti' comes from a Maasai word for 'endless plains'."

### 3 · The river crossing — AI-M (atmosphere)
- **Picture:** Wildebeest plunge down a muddy riverbank and swim across in a churning line; spray and dust in warm light. No people. (Dramatic, not gory: no predators catching anything.)
- **Narration:** "Along the way, they leap into rivers full of crocodiles, just to reach fresh grass on the other side."

### 4 · Ngorongoro Crater — LAYER (pan)
- **Picture:** A vast green crater floor ringed by steep forested walls; a soda lake with flamingos as a pink smudge; tiny herds; clouds spilling over the rim.
- **Narration:** "Ngorongoro Crater is a collapsed volcano nearly twenty kilometres across, with a whole world of animals living inside."
- **Fact card:** "It's one of the best places in Africa to see a black rhino."

### 4b · Maasai country — LAYER
- **Picture:** Late afternoon on the highland grassland near the crater: a Maasai homestead of low round houses inside a circular thorn fence, cattle grazing with bells, people in red and blue cloth painted as loose figures with no facial detail. The traveler stands at a respectful distance beside a local guide (also a loose figure). Long shadows, calm. No dancing or posed "show".
- **Narration:** "The Maasai have herded cattle across these highlands for generations. Many communities welcome visitors, with a local guide, on their own terms."
- **Local tip:** "Visit through a community-run program, and always ask before taking photos."
- **Care notes:** no caricature, no costume clichés beyond everyday dress, no jumping-dance cliché, nobody posed for the camera; the scene shows ordinary daily life.

### 5 · Tarangire's baobabs — LAYER
- **Picture:** Giant baobab trees in dry golden grass; an elephant family walks slowly across the foreground as a cut-out layer.
- **Narration:** "In Tarangire, huge baobab trees, some more than a thousand years old, shade herds of elephants."

### 6 · Kilimanjaro — AI-K (hero)
- **Picture:** Sunrise above a sea of clouds. The traveler, seen from behind on a rocky trail with trekking poles, looks up at the snow-capped summit glowing pink.
- **Narration:** "Kilimanjaro is Africa's highest mountain, at five thousand eight hundred and ninety-five metres. And you can walk to the top, with no ropes."
- **Say it:** "Pole pole — slowly, slowly" (the climbers' motto).

### 7 · Five climates in one climb — LAYER (tilt)
- **Picture:** A tall cross-section painting of the mountain's slopes: rainforest at the bottom, then heather moorland, alpine desert, and glaciers at the top. The camera tilts up through them.
- **Narration:** "On the way up, you pass through rainforest, moorland and desert, and finally reach ice."

### 8 · Stone Town, Zanzibar — LAYER
- **Picture:** A narrow Stone Town lane with whitewashed walls and an intricately carved wooden door with brass studs; the traveler small in the lane. The door is a foreground layer that the camera eases toward.
- **Narration:** "Off the coast, Zanzibar's Stone Town is a maze of carved wooden doors, shaped by African, Arab, Indian and European history."
- **Fact card (History):** "Stone Town was once a center of the East African slave trade. A cathedral now stands on the site of the old slave market."
- *(The Freddie Mercury fact was dropped to make room; the engine shows one card per beat.)*

### 9 · The Spice Island — AI-M (atmosphere)
- **Picture:** Close-up of hands holding fresh spices on a farm: clove buds, a split nutmeg with its red mace, cinnamon bark, vanilla pods; leaves moving in a breeze. Hands only.
- **Narration:** "Zanzibar is nicknamed the Spice Island: cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon and vanilla all grow here."
- **Say it:** "Asante — thank you"

### 10 · A dhow at sunset — AI-K (hero)
- **Picture:** A wooden dhow with a big triangular sail glides over turquoise water at sunset; the traveler sits on deck, seen from behind, her scarf in the wind.
- **Narration:** "And at sunset, wooden dhows still sail these waters, just as they have for centuries."

### 11 · Traveler's notes — CODE
- **Picture:** The journal page fills with ticked notes.
- **Notes:**
  - **Best time:** June–October (dry season) for safaris; January–February for the migration's calving season.
  - **Budget:** $$–$$$; park fees make safaris the big cost.
  - **Money:** Tanzanian shilling; US dollars are widely used for safaris and park fees.
  - **Language:** Swahili and English.
  - **Respect:** ask before photographing people, dress modestly in Stone Town, and never feed wildlife.
- **Narration:** "Come in the dry season for safaris, carry some shillings, and always ask before taking someone's photo."

### 12 · Next stop — CODE
- **Picture:** The route line runs south-west from Tanzania to a waterfall sketch on the Zambia–Zimbabwe border.
- **Narration:** "Next stop: Victoria Falls."
- **On screen:** "STOP 04 → VICTORIA FALLS"; stamp "VISITED · TANZANIA".

**Narration:** about 255 words across 14 beats. **Estimated running time:** ~112 s once each scene is timed to its line.

## Facts to verify before publishing

- [ ] Laetoli footprints: about 3.6–3.7 million years old, preserved in volcanic ash; "still here" (they were reburied to protect them, with a replica on display, so the wording says they survive, not that you can see them on site)
- [ ] Great Migration: roughly 1.5 million wildebeest (plus zebra and gazelle); "Serengeti" from the Maasai word for "endless plains"
- [ ] River crossings (Mara/Grumeti) happen mainly July–October
- [ ] Ngorongoro Crater ≈ 19–20 km across; black rhino viewing
- [ ] Some Tarangire baobabs are over 1,000 years old
- [ ] Kilimanjaro height 5,895 m; walkable "trekking peak", no technical climbing
- [ ] Climate zones on Kilimanjaro (rainforest, heath/moorland, alpine desert, arctic summit)
- [ ] Stone Town UNESCO status; the slave-market history and the Anglican Cathedral (Christ Church) built on the former market site
- [ ] Maasai beat wording: pastoralists in northern Tanzania and southern Kenya; community-run visits
- [ ] Spices grown on Zanzibar (cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, vanilla)
- [ ] Best-season, money and etiquette advice
