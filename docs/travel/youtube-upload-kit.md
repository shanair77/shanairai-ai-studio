## Suggested order and schedule

1. **Day 1 · Season 1 long video.** It goes first so every Short can link to it.
2. **Days 1–7 · one Short a day, in travel order** (Morocco first). A steady daily rhythm gives each Short its own day in the Shorts feed, and the series story ("Next stop: …") plays out in order.
3. After each Short is up, set its **Related video** to the Season 1 video (Shorts edit page → Related video). Viewers then get a link from the Short to the full journey.

## Settings for every upload

- **Audience:** "Made for kids" is your legal declaration. The series is written for travellers and adult learners, so "No, it's not made for kids" fits it, but it is your call.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** this is a stylised watercolour cartoon, not realistic footage, so YouTube's label is not expected to apply. Read the "Disclosing altered or synthetic content" help page once before the first upload.
- **Category:** Travel & Events. **Video language:** English.
- **Playlist:** create "Postcards from Africa · Season 1" and add all 8 videos (long video first, then Shorts 01–07).
- **Captions:** YouTube's automatic English captions work well on this narration; check the place names (Jemaa el-Fnaa, Mosi-oa-Tunya, Tsodilo, Deadvlei, Bo-Kaap) after they generate.
